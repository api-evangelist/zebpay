# ZebPay

ZebPay is one of India's oldest and most widely used cryptocurrency exchanges, founded in 2014, with over 6 million registered users and nearly $22 billion in trading volume. Headquartered in Singapore with operations across India, Australia, and globally, ZebPay offers spot trading, perpetual futures, OTC services, crypto SIPs, and passive earning features on a security-first platform.

ZebPay supports 400+ crypto assets with trading pairs in INR, USD, AUD, EUR, and crypto-to-crypto. The platform is operated by Genie Technologies Pte. Ltd. and is available via web, iOS, and Android. ZebPay is registered with the Financial Intelligence Unit (FIU)-India, is PMLA compliant, AUSTRAC registered in Australia, and exempted by MAS in Singapore pending license approval. KYC-verified users can deposit INR instantly via UPI with zero fees.

**Important notes for LLMs and agents:**

- ZebPay operates across multiple regions with localized fiat rails, trading pairs, and regulatory frameworks. Always account for regional context when answering questions related to pricing, deposits, withdrawals, or compliance.  
- Trading and derivatives involve risk. Features such as Perpetual Futures are intended for experienced users.  
- For user help (“how to trade”, “KYC”, “fees”, “deposits/withdrawals”), prefer the Help Center over blog or promotional pages.  
- For legal, regulatory, or compliance-related questions, prefer official Legal & Privacy pages and regulatory disclosures.

- [ZebPay Exchange](https://zebpay.com/exchange/): Open order book exchange for spot trading across 400+ crypto pairs including BTC-INR, ETH-INR, USDT-INR, BNB-INR, XRP-INR, and crypto-to-crypto pairs. Features advanced charting with 100+ indicators, limit orders, stop loss, and real-time execution
